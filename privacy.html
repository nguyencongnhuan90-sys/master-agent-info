Privacy Policy

MASTER_AGENT Local is a personal desktop application used only by its owner with the owner's own Google/YouTube accounts.

  • The app requests access to the owner's YouTube channels only to upload and schedule the owner's own videos and to read the owner's own channel statistics and comments.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are never sent to any server operated by the developer or to any third party.
  • The app does not collect, sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
